Officer Career Journey

Breadth. Specialisation.
Mastery. Leadership.

Your career develops through three connected stories: how you grow as a leader, how professional education prepares you, and where you may serve.

01 How you grow 02 How you are developed 03 Where you may serve
01
(Second Lieutenant to Captain)
Age: 19

How you grow

Build strong foundations.

Learn the fundamentals of command, build confidence in the Artillery profession and gain breadth through operational and battalion staff appointments.

02

How you are developed

Professional education prepares you for command and appointment.

Army Professional Military Education

Builds command, tactical and staff foundations.

  • Joint Foundation Course
  • Company Tactics Course
  • Company Command Course

Courses aligned to your appointments

Artillery courses are undertaken according to the roles and domains you pursue.

  • Basic Target Acquisition Commanders Course For officers preparing for appointments within the FATA domain.
  • Sense-Strike Mission Team Course For officers preparing to undertake appointments such as SSMT Lead.
  • Integrated Strike Course For officers taking up specialist battlestaff appointments.
  • Advanced Artillery Officer Course Deepens the professional foundations required for later appointments.
