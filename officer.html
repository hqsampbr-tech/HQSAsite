Entry Requirements and Benefits

Basic Requirements

Singapore Citizen or Singapore PR intending to take up citizenship.

Physically and medically fit.

Academic Qualifications

‘A’ Level or Diploma.

SAVER

Retirement benefits designed to provide financial security beyond service.

Contributions begin from the first year of service until the SAVER end date.

See Salary and Full Benefits

Flexible Benefits System (FLEX)

FLEX quantum is tiered based on reckonable length of service.

Stage 1: $800 per year, up to 10 years of service.

Stage 2: $1,000 per year, beyond 10 years of service.

Leave

Annual leave of up to 21 days, with additional leave schemes for different life stages.
