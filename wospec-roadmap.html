How you grow

Novice

Build breadth and technical mastery.

Build strong foundations in an Artillery platform, lead frontline teams and progressively broaden across multiple systems.

02

How you are developed

Platform mastery expands into professional breadth.

Army Development

  • Basic Warfighter – Platoon Sergeant
  • Warfighter – Company Sergeant Major Course

Artillery Development

  • Artillery Specialist Cadet Course aligned to the assigned platform, such as ASCC (HIMARS)
  • Cross-trained in two Artillery platforms by SSG–MSG
  • Cross-trained in a third platform by MSG–3WO
  • Advanced Artillery Specialist Course
  • Artillery Expert Programme in preparation for the next career phase
